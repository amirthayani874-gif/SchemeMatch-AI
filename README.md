# SchemeMatch AI

## AI-Driven Government Scheme Matching

SchemeMatch AI is an AI-powered platform designed to help entrepreneurs discover suitable government schemes based on their personal and business profiles.

The system analyzes user information such as age, location, entrepreneur category, business type, business stage, income, investment requirements, and other relevant factors to recommend government schemes that may be suitable for them.

## Features

### AI-Based Scheme Matching

SchemeMatch AI analyzes the entrepreneur's profile and generates personalized scheme recommendations with match scores and relevant eligibility information.

### Explainable Recommendations

The platform provides reasons behind scheme recommendations so users can understand why a particular scheme was matched to their profile.

### AI Assistant

The integrated AI Assistant allows users to ask questions about their recommended government schemes and receive simple, personalized guidance.

### Multilingual Support

The platform supports multiple languages:

* English
* Tamil
* Hindi
* Malayalam

Users can interact with the platform and AI Assistant in their preferred language.

### Profile Management

Users can create and store multiple entrepreneur profiles. Stored profiles can be selected when using the platform, making it easier to manage information for different entrepreneurs.

Users can also delete profiles when they are no longer required.

### Document Readiness

The Document Readiness feature helps users track the documents required for their recommended schemes.

Users can:

* View required documents
* Upload documents
* Check document availability
* Remove uploaded documents
* Track document readiness for each scheme

Uploaded documents are associated with the selected profile and scheme.

### Real-Time Notifications

SchemeMatch AI provides notification support for different stored profiles.

Notifications can be used to keep users informed about relevant scheme updates and changes.

Users can also disable notifications for a profile when they are not required.

### Scheme Comparison

The Compare page allows users to compare their top two recommended schemes based on relevant scheme information, helping them understand the differences between the recommendations.

### Official Scheme Links

The platform provides links to official government scheme sources so users can verify eligibility requirements and obtain additional information before applying.

### Multiple Profile Support

The platform is designed to support multiple entrepreneur profiles. Each profile can have its own scheme recommendations, document readiness information, and notification settings.

## How It Works

1. Create or select an entrepreneur profile.
2. Enter the required personal and business information.
3. Analyze the profile against available government schemes.
4. View personalized scheme recommendations and match scores.
5. Compare recommended schemes.
6. Ask questions using the AI Assistant.
7. Check the documents required for each recommended scheme.
8. Upload and manage required documents.
9. Receive notifications about relevant scheme updates.
10. Verify the final eligibility and requirements through the official government sources.

## Disclaimer

SchemeMatch AI provides indicative recommendations based on the information provided by the user.

Match scores and recommendations should not be considered official eligibility decisions. Users should verify the latest eligibility criteria, documentation requirements, application procedures, and other details through the respective official government portals before applying.
